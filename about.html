From Certification Journey to Community Service

NHN-TECHROOM wasn't built as a business—it was built as a mission.
After achieving my Expert-level certifications, I realized the biggest barrier for engineers isn't intelligence or effort; it's the high cost of enterprise hardware.

The Story Behind the Lab

To master technologies like Aruba AOS-CX, VSX, and Arista EOS, I spent years curating a high-performance home lab. I hand-picked every server and optimized every virtual topology to ensure they mirrored the complex environments of global enterprises.

Now that I've reached my certification goals, I don't want this powerful setup to sit idle. I am opening up my personal infrastructure to the engineering community to provide affordable, high-performance lab space for those who are on their own path to Expert status.

"My goal is to provide the rack space I wish I had when I started: reliable, pre-configured, and accessible to everyone."
